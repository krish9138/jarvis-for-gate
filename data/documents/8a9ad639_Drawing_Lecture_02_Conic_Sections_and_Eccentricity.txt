## 1. Conic Sections: Generation from Right Circular Cone
A right circular double cone (two nappes joined at apex) cut by a section plane yields different conic sections depending on the cutting plane angle $\theta$ relative to the cone axis angle $\alpha$ (semi-apex angle) or generator:

| Cutting Plane Condition | Resulting Conic Section | Key Geometric Characteristics |
|---|---|---|
| **Perpendicular to Axis** ($\theta = 90^\circ$) | **Circle** | Symmetrical closed curve, eccentricity $e = 0$ |
| **Inclined to Axis & cuts all generators** ($\theta > \alpha$) | **Ellipse** | Closed oval curve, eccentricity $e < 1$ |
| **Parallel to one generator line** ($\theta = \alpha$) | **Parabola** | Open symmetrical curve, eccentricity $e = 1$ |
| **Parallel to / cuts at angle smaller than semi-apex angle** ($\theta < \alpha$) | **Hyperbola** | Open curve with two separate branches, $e > 1$ |
| **Parallel to Axis of Right-Angled Cone** ($2\alpha = 90^\circ$) | **Rectangular Hyperbola** | Included angle between asymptotes is $90^\circ$, $e = \sqrt{2}$ |
| **Passing through Apex** | **Straight Intersecting Lines / Triangle** | Degenerate conic |

## 2. Conic as a Locus of a Point (Focus-Directrix Property)
- **Definition**: A conic is the locus of a point moving in a plane such that the ratio of its distance from a fixed point (**Focus**, $F$) to its perpendicular distance from a fixed straight line (**Directrix**, $DD'$) is always constant.
- **Eccentricity ($e$)**:
  $$e = \frac{\text{Distance of point from Focus}}{\text{Distance of point from Directrix}} = \frac{PF}{PM}$$

### Eccentricity Values Matrix:
- **Circle**: $e = 0$
- **Ellipse**: $0 < e < 1$ (Distance to focus < Distance to directrix)
- **Parabola**: $e = 1$ (Distance to focus = Distance to directrix)
- **Hyperbola**: $e > 1$ (Distance to focus > Distance to directrix)
- **Rectangular Hyperbola**: $e = \sqrt{2} \approx 1.414$

### Important Conic Terminology:
1. **Directrix ($DD'$)**: Fixed straight line perpendicular to the principal axis.
2. **Axis ($CC'$)**: Line passing through the focus and perpendicular to the directrix.
3. **Vertex ($V$)**: Point of intersection of the conic curve with its axis. The vertex is the point on the curve **closest to the focus**.
4. **Focus ($F$)**: Fixed focal point on the axis.
5. **Ordinate**: Line segment perpendicular to the axis from any point on the curve to the axis.
6. **Double Ordinate**: Chord perpendicular to the axis.
7. **Latus Rectum ($LL'$)**: Double ordinate passing directly through the focus.

## 3. Special Definitions & Formulas for Ellipse and Hyperbola
### Ellipse (Sum of Focal Distances Constant):
- **Definition**: Locus of a point such that the **sum of its distances from two fixed points (foci $F_1, F_2$) is constant and equal to the Major Axis ($2a$)**.
  $$F_1 P + F_2 P = 2a = \text{Major Axis}$$
- **Distance from end of minor axis to focus** $= \frac{1}{2} \times \text{Major Axis} = a$.
- **Formulas for Eccentricity ($e$) of an Ellipse**:
  1. $e = \frac{\text{Distance between foci}}{\text{Length of major axis}} = \frac{F_1 F_2}{2a}$
  2. $e = \frac{\text{Length of major axis}}{\text{Distance between directrices}} = \frac{2a}{MN}$
  3. $e = \sqrt{\frac{\text{Distance between foci}}{\text{Distance between directrices}}} = \sqrt{\frac{F_1 F_2}{MN}}$
  - *Numerical Example*: Distance between directrices $MN = 245\text{ mm}$, distance between foci $F_1 F_2 = 20\text{ mm}$:
    $$2a = \sqrt{F_1 F_2 \times MN} = \sqrt{20 \times 245} = \sqrt{4900} = 70\text{ mm}$$

### Hyperbola (Difference of Focal Distances Constant):
- **Definition**: Locus of a point such that the **absolute difference of its distances from two fixed points (foci $F_1, F_2$) is constant and equal to the distance between vertices ($V_1 V_2 = \text{Transverse Axis}$)**.
  $$|F_1 P - F_2 P| = V_1 V_2 = \text{Transverse Axis}$$

### Engineering Applications of Conics:
- **Parabola**: Path of a projectile under gravity; parabolic reflectors, bridge suspension cables.
- **Ellipse**: Planetary orbits (Kepler's Law); arch bridges, elliptical gears.
- **Rectangular Hyperbola**: Boyle's Law of gas expansion ($P \cdot V = C$), cooling tower profiles.
