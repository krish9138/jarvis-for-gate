## 1. Conic Sections: Fundamental Conical Angles & Relations
A right circular cone is generated by rotating a right-angled triangle about its altitude or rotating a generator line about an axis at a fixed angle.

### Key Angle Definitions:
- **$2\theta$**: Apex Angle of the cone.
- **$\theta$**: Semi-apex Angle (angle between the axis and generator).
- **$\phi$**: Angle between the **Cutting Plane (C.P.) and the Axis** of the cone.
- **$\alpha$**: Angle between the **Generator ($G$) and the Base/Ground** ($\alpha = 90^\circ - \theta$).
- **$\beta$**: Angle between the **Cutting Plane (C.P.) and the Base/Ground** ($\beta = 90^\circ - \phi$).

### The 4 Standard Conic Conditions Based on Cutting Angles ($\theta$ vs $\phi$):
1. **Circle**:
   - Cutting plane is **perpendicular to the axis** ($\phi = 90^\circ$, parallel to base).
2. **Ellipse**:
   - Cutting plane cuts all generators on one side of apex.
   - Angle relation: **$\theta < \phi$** (Angle with axis is greater than semi-apex angle) or **$\beta < \alpha$** (Angle with base is less than generator base angle).
3. **Parabola**:
   - Cutting plane is **parallel to one generator line**.
   - Angle relation: **$\theta = \phi$** or **$\beta = \alpha$**.
4. **Hyperbola**:
   - Cutting plane cuts both nappes of the double cone or angle with axis is steeper than generator.
   - Angle relation: **$\theta > \phi$** (Angle with axis is less than semi-apex angle) or **$\beta > \alpha$**.

---

## 2. Special Case: Rectangular Hyperbola
- Occurs when a section plane is **parallel to and away from the axis** of a **right-angled cone ($2\theta = 90^\circ \implies \theta = 45^\circ$)**.
- The included angle between the **asymptotes is strictly $90^\circ$**.
- Follows the mathematical expansion law:
  $$P \cdot V = C \quad \text{or} \quad x \cdot y = C$$
- **Asymptote**: A straight line that continuously approaches a given curve but does not meet it at any finite distance.

---

## 3. Degenerate Conics (When Cutting Plane Passes Through the Apex)
When the cutting section plane passes directly through the **Apex / Vertex** of the double cone, the conic sections degenerate into simpler geometric figures:

| Normal Conic Form | Condition Passing Through Apex | Resulting Degenerate Figure |
|---|---|---|
| **Ellipse / Circle** | $\phi > \theta$ passing through apex | **A Single Point** (the apex itself) |
| **Parabola** | $\phi = \theta$ (tangent to generator through apex) | **A Single Straight Line** (one generator) |
| **Hyperbola** | $\phi < \theta$ (cutting both nappes through apex) | **Two Straight Intersecting Lines** |
| **Solid Cone Cut through Apex & Base** | Slices through apex to the base | **An Isosceles Triangle** |

## 4. Lecture 01 - Key Practice Questions & Solutions
1. **Right circular cone cut by section plane meeting axis greater than semi-apex angle ($\phi > \theta$)**: $\rightarrow$ **(a) Ellipse**.
2. **Right circular cone cut by plane parallel to axis**: $\rightarrow$ **(a) Hyperbola**.
3. **Right circular cone cut by plane where angle between asymptotes is $90^\circ$**: $\rightarrow$ **(d) Rectangular hyperbola**.
4. **Right circular hollow cone cut by plane through apex and base**: $\rightarrow$ **(d) Intersecting lines**.
5. **Right circular solid cone cut by plane through apex and base**: $\rightarrow$ **(d) Triangle** (special degenerate case of hyperbola).
