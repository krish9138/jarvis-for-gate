## 1. Reference Planes & 4-Quadrant System
Orthographic projection uses two principal planes of projection:
1. **Horizontal Plane (H.P.)**: Rotated $90^\circ$ clockwise to lie flat on the drawing sheet.
2. **Vertical Plane (V.P.)**: Remains stationary in front of the observer.
3. **Reference Line ($xy$)**: The line of intersection between H.P. and V.P.

### The 4 Quadrants Matrix:
| Quadrant | Position Relative to H.P. & V.P. | Elevation / Front View ($p'$) | Plan / Top View ($p$) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1st Quadrant** | **Above H.P. & In front of V.P.** | **Above $xy$ line** | **Below $xy$ line** |
| **2nd Quadrant** | **Above H.P. & Behind V.P.** | **Above $xy$ line** | **Above $xy$ line** (both overlap above $xy$) |
| **3rd Quadrant** | **Below H.P. & Behind V.P.** | **Below $xy$ line** | **Above $xy$ line** |
| **4th Quadrant** | **Below H.P. & In front of V.P.** | **Below $xy$ line** | **Below $xy$ line** (both overlap below $xy$) |

### Fundamental Rules for Point Projections:
- **Distance from H.P.** is always seen and measured in the **Front View (Elevation)** above or below $xy$.
- **Distance from V.P.** is always seen and measured in the **Top View (Plan)** above or below $xy$.
- **Distance from Profile Plane (P.P.)** is the horizontal distance between the projector of the point and the reference line $x_1 y_1$.
- **Point lying ON the H.P.**: Its distance from H.P. is zero $\rightarrow$ its Front View ($p'$) lies **on the $xy$ line**.
- **Point lying ON the V.P.**: Its distance from V.P. is zero $\rightarrow$ its Top View ($p$) lies **on the $xy$ line**.

## 2. 3D Spatial Geometry & Distance Formulas
Let a point $P$ in the 1st Quadrant have:
- Distance from H.P. = $h$ (Height $\rightarrow$ Front View height $p'$)
- Distance from V.P. = $d$ (Depth $\rightarrow$ Top View distance $p$)
- Distance from P.P. = $w$ (Distance from $x_1 y_1$)

1. **Distance between Front View ($p'$) and Top View ($p$)**:
   $$\text{Distance} = h + d$$
2. **Distance between Front View ($p'$) and Side View ($p''$)**:
   $$\text{Distance} = w + d$$
3. **Shortest Distance from Top View ($p$) to Side View ($p''$)**:
   $$\text{Distance} = \sqrt{(h + d)^2 + (w + d)^2}$$
4. **Shortest Distance from Point $P$ to the Intersection of H.P. and V.P. (the $xy$ line)**:
   $$\text{Shortest Distance to } xy = \sqrt{h^2 + d^2}$$

## 3. First Angle vs Third Angle Projection Standards
- **BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards)** and **ISO** recommend **First Angle Projection** (`symbol: frustum of cone with base circle to right`).
- **Relative Positions**:
  - *1st Angle*: Observer $\rightarrow$ **Object** $\rightarrow$ Plane of Projection (Object is placed in between).
    - Right Side View is drawn on the **Left of Elevation (FV)**.
    - Top View is drawn **Below Elevation (FV)**.
  - *3rd Angle*: Observer $\rightarrow$ **Plane of Projection** $\rightarrow$ Object (Plane is placed in between).
