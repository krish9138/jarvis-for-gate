## 1. High-Yield ESE Exam Principles & Formulas
### A. Representative Fraction (R.F.)
- $RF = \frac{\text{Length of drawing}}{\text{Actual length}}$
- *Example*: If $1.5\text{ inches} = 1\text{ foot}$ ($1\text{ foot} = 12\text{ inches}$):
  $$RF = \frac{1.5}{1 \times 12} = \frac{1.5}{12} = \frac{1}{8}$$

### B. Oblique Projections: Cavalier vs Cabinet
- Oblique projection: Projectors are parallel to each other and inclined at an angle (typically $30^\circ, 45^\circ$, or $60^\circ$) to the projection plane.
- Front face parallel to projection plane retains **true shape and size**.
- **Cavalier Projection**: Receding lines are drawn to **full size scale** ($1:1$).
- **Cabinet Projection**: Receding lines are foreshortened to **half size scale** ($1:2$ or $0.5$) to reduce optical distortion.

### C. Conic Sections from Cone Cutting Planes
1. **Circle**: Cutting plane perpendicular to axis (parallel to base).
2. **Ellipse**: Cutting plane inclined to axis cutting all generators on one side of apex.
3. **Parabola**: Cutting plane parallel to one of the generator lines (angle with axis equals semi-apex angle).
4. **Hyperbola**: Cutting plane parallel to axis or cutting both nappes of a double cone.
5. **Rectangular Hyperbola**: Cutting plane parallel to axis of a right-angled cone ($2\theta = 90^\circ$); curve traced where product of distances from two perpendicular asymptotes is constant ($P \cdot V = C$).

### D. Traces of Lines & Planes
- **Horizontal Trace (H.T.)**: Point where line meets / penetrates Horizontal Plane (HP).
- **Vertical Trace (V.T.)**: Point where line meets / penetrates Vertical Plane (VP).
- **Line parallel to both HP and VP**: Has **NO trace**.
- **Line inclined to HP and parallel to VP**: Has **only H.T. but no V.T.**
- **Line inclined to VP and parallel to HP**: Has **only V.T. but no H.T.**
- **Line perpendicular to HP**: H.T. coincides with top view (a point); has **no V.T.**
- **Line perpendicular to VP**: V.T. coincides with front view (a point); has **no H.T.**
- **Traces of a Plane**: The lines in which a plane meets the reference planes (HP and VP).

### E. Loci and Special Curves
- **Hypocycloid (Straight Line Special Case)**: If the radius of generating circle rolling inside a directing circle is **half the radius of the directing circle** ($r = \frac{R}{2}$), the locus of a point on the circumference is a **straight line** (diameter of the directing circle).
- **Archimedean Spiral**: Locus of a point moving along a pendulum rod at uniform speed while the pendulum oscillates.
- **Highway Interchanges**: Designed using transition curves based on **Hyperbola / Spiral / Lemniscate**.
